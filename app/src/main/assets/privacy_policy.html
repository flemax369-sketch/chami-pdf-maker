Chami - Img to PDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 Nov 2026

1. We do NOT collect, upload, or sell your files. All PDFs, images, text, and resumes stay on your phone and are processed locally.

2. We do NOT ask for an account, email, or phone number. No signup or registration is required.

3. Permissions used only when you use that feature:

  • Camera: Used only when scanning documents.
  • Photos / Media: Used only to select pictures you want to convert.
  • Storage: Used only to save PDFs to your local storage.

4. No ads in v1. This app shows no ads and uses no advertising ID.

5. OCR runs on-device. Text recognition is performed locally using on-device ML Kit. Text from your documents is never sent to any server.

6. Contact & Data Deletion: In-app Settings > Help. Deleting the app removes all your local data.
